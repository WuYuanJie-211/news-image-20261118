# The World — Wednesday, 18 November 2026

One image that sums up the world's news of Wednesday 18 November 2026,
laid out as a newspaper front page. The judge has no news feed, so the
image is self-contained: the date is in the masthead and every story is
told in words on the page itself.

## What's on the page

- **Lead:** Trump lands in China for the historic three-way summit with
  Xi and Putin at APEC in Shenzhen — the first meeting of its kind.
- **Iran** says its drones struck a US base in Kuwait (Ali Al Salem),
  with attacks also reported in Bahrain and on a ship in the Strait of
  Hormuz, and Houthi drone fire at a Saudi oil pipeline.
- **Gaza:** Israel begins "focused operations" in three central Gaza
  areas; Trump's peace board says both sides failed ceasefire pledges.
- **Cyclone Akvan** strengthens as it nears India's Odisha coast.
- **Quad** leaders meet in Manila for the first time in over two years;
  the US and the Philippines sign a major trade deal.
- **Washington:** Peltola's win puts Democrats on course for a 56–44
  Senate majority.
- **Also today:** GTA VI launches at midnight, a magnitude 6.0 quake
  hits Papua New Guinea, Britain sanctions firms linked to West Bank
  settlements.

## How it was made

`build/front.html` is the front page as plain HTML and CSS (DejaVu
Serif/Sans, system fonts, no network, no images). It was rendered to
`entry.png` (1024×1024) with a headless Chromium screenshot, and the
text was read back and checked by eye — dates, headlines and every
story are legible, nothing is cut off. No text was generated by an
image model; the wording is the news as reported on the day.

## The program

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png` — a pure byte copy, so two runs write identical
bytes. Outside the grader, `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` stands in for `/output`:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
